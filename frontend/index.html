1.00g EARTH GRAVITY

Buoyancy Pulls Fresh Oxygen In

On Earth, hot combustion gases are lighter than cool air. They rise rapidly, creating an upward plume that draws fresh oxygen into the flame base.

Upward spread is 3.8×–5.2× faster than downward spread due to buoyant convection.

NASA SPACE APPS 2026 · FLAME IN FREEFALL

Fire does not rise in orbit. Without buoyancy, it creeps spherically and fills cabins with invisible toxicants.

Full 3D reacting CFD takes hours per run and rarely models downstream crew health. Ashes of Microgravity couples reduced-order flame-spread physics (de Ris / Quintiere), a 2D thermal-pyrolysis PDE solver, a 41-feature PyTorch ensemble, and a dynamic cabin toxicology ODE (ISO 13571 FED & CFK COHb%) to screen both material flammability and crew survival in milliseconds.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS & NASA-STD-7009A ALIGNMENT

Scope & Credibility Boundaries

  • 3 Flight-Validated Fuels of 118: Direct microgravity telemetry exists for SIBAL, PMMA, and Nomex 450 (used as proxy for Saffire-IV Nomex HT9040). Other 115 materials are thermophysical extrapolations with explicit applicability scores (0–100).
  • Single-Point Regimes: Low-pressure radiative feedback (55 kPa, Saffire-V S4) and near-LOI damping (26% O₂, Saffire-IV S2) rely on single runs and carry wider uncertainty.
  • Surrogate Is an Emulator: It reproduces the physics engine (~2.2% avg gap on plates/fabrics, 16–26% on low-flow PMMA rods) and is not independently validated against flight data.
  • Well-Mixed Cabin Assumption: Toxicology ODE models uniform cabin mixing (ISS 106 m³, Orion 9.3 m³, Gateway 60 m³, Mars 250 m³) with an assumed 2.1× 0g CO/soot multiplier.
  • Disclaimer: Research and educational tool only — not clinical guidance or NASA-STD-6001B hardware certification.
